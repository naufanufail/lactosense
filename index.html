01 — Cardiometabolic Disorders

When the body's balance breaks

Cardiometabolic disorders are interconnected conditions that disrupt the heart, blood vessels, and metabolic system — often developing silently for years.

♥
CARDIOVASCULAR

Heart Disease

Conditions affecting the heart's structure and function — including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and arrhythmias. Globally responsible for over 17 million deaths per year.

#1 cause of death worldwide

METABOLIC

Type 2 Diabetes

A chronic metabolic condition where cells become resistant to insulin, causing elevated blood glucose. Affects 537 million adults worldwide and rising rapidly.

537M affected globally
⚖
SYNDROME

Metabolic Syndrome

A cluster of conditions — abdominal obesity, high blood pressure, high blood sugar, and abnormal lipid levels — that occur together, increasing your risk of heart disease and stroke.

1 in 3 adults in some regions

03 — The Lactate Signal

Why lactate tells the story

Lactate is your body's metabolic distress signal — produced when cells can't get enough oxygen or metabolize glucose normally.

✓ NORMAL PATHWAY
⬡Glucose
→
◈Pyruvate
→
⚡ATP

Cells efficiently convert glucose → energy with adequate oxygen.

⚠ DISRUPTED PATHWAY
⬡Glucose
→
◈Pyruvate
⟶
⬡Lactate ↑

When oxygen is limited or metabolism is impaired, lactate accumulates as a byproduct.

🩺 Lactate crosses into sweat in concentrations proportional to blood levels — making sweat an accessible, non-invasive window into your metabolic state.

05 — The Limitation

Why lactate alone isn't enough

Powerful as it is, sweat lactate is one piece of a complex puzzle. Robust screening requires multiple data dimensions.

🔁

Confounding Variables

Exercise, diet, stress, and medications all independently elevate lactate, making isolated readings ambiguous.

📍

No Disease Specificity

Lactate elevation is a non-specific signal. Without supporting biomarkers, a definitive diagnosis cannot be made.

🧠

Missing the Cognitive Link

Cardiometabolic disorders significantly impair cognitive function — a dimension lactate alone cannot capture.

📈

Threshold Variability

"Normal" lactate ranges differ by age, fitness level, and individual baseline — static thresholds risk misclassification.

💡 Solution: Pairing sweat lactate with cognitive performance testing creates a two-dimensional biomarker profile — metabolic + neurological — substantially improving diagnostic utility.

10 — Scientific Foundation

The research behind it

Grounded in peer-reviewed literature across analytical chemistry, bioelectronics, and clinical metabolomics.

⚗

Lactate Oxidase Catalysis

LOx enzyme catalyzes: Lactate + O₂ → Pyruvate + H₂O₂. The hydrogen peroxide is electrochemically oxidized at the working electrode, producing a current proportional to lactate concentration.

📄 Heikenfeld et al., Lab Chip 2018
💧

Sweat-Blood Correlation

Sweat lactate correlates with blood lactate across physiological ranges (0.5–10 mmol/L), though sweat concentration is typically 1.5–3× higher. Calibration algorithms correct for this offset.

📄 Mena-Bravo et al., Anal. Chim. Acta 2014
🧬

Cognitive-Metabolic Link

Insulin resistance and impaired cerebral glucose metabolism co-occur in T2DM and CVD, reducing processing speed, working memory, and executive function — measurable via validated cognitive tasks.

📄 Biessels & Despa, Nat Rev Neurol 2018
📡

Wearable Integration

Modern flexible electronics enable real-time sweat sampling with microfluidic channels, on-chip analog front-ends, and Bluetooth transmission — enabling continuous ambulatory monitoring.

📄 Gao et al., Nature 2016
